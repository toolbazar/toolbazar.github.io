GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Is Tool Bazar free?

Yes. The tools on this site are free to use. Some large files or complex conversions may be limited by your device and browser.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

Supported PDF operations run locally in your browser. Tool Bazar does not send your selected files to its own server. Third-party CDN libraries are loaded to power the tools.

Can I use Tool Bazar on my phone?

Yes. The site is responsive and works in modern mobile browsers, although large documents may work better on a desktop.

Will compression always make my PDF smaller?

No. Results depend on the source file. Image-based compression can reduce size, but it may flatten selectable text and affect quality. Review the output before using it.
